# 6_Cost-and-Regions — storyboard

**Length:** ~3:00 · **Recorded on:** production AADB · **Music:** drive · **Narrator:** Andrew HD

**Asset:** `assets/clinic-booking-first-draft.json` (8 services, $118.83/mo in East US 2).
Region prices come from fixed catalog snapshots, so the numbers quoted here are stable (Canada Central $117.91, Brazil South $143.94, App Service P1 v3 × 2 = $226.30, total $275.78).
